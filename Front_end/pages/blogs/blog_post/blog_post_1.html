2021 STYLE GUIDE: THE BIGGEST FALL TRENDS

A great pair of boots can completely change an outfit, and lately I have been reaching for simple black boots more than anything else. They are comfortable, versatile and somehow manage to make even the most relaxed looks feel more put together.These black boots are one of those wardrobe pieces that you can wear again and again without getting tired of them. I especially love them styled with soft neutral fabrics because the darker color creates such a nice contrast. They give the outfit a little edge while still keeping everything clean and minimal.The best thing about investing in a classic shoe is how many different ways you can wear it. With jeans, skirts, tailored trousers or oversized coats, a simple black boot always seems to work. For me, this is definitely one of those essentials that makes getting dressed so much easier.
